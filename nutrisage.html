How TDEE is Calculated

NutriSage uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation — the most accurate formula for estimating basal metabolic rate (BMR). Your BMR is then multiplied by your activity factor to get Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE). Your goal offset is applied on top. This is the gold standard approach used by clinical dietitians.
